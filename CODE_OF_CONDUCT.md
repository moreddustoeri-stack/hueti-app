  # Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project supports a small childcare and community-oriented environment.

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, safe and supportive experience for everyone involved.

We especially value patience, empathy and constructive communication, as some contributors and staff members come from multilingual, socially or integration-challenged backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

Participants and contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively
- show empathy and professionalism
- support an inclusive and discrimination-free environment
- respect the privacy and dignity of children, families and staff
- avoid harassment, intimidation or abusive behavior
- contribute in a way that supports accessibility and collaboration

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- harassment or personal attacks
- discrimination based on ethnicity, language, religion, gender, disability or background
- hateful, sexualized or abusive language
- intimidation or deliberate disruption
- sharing sensitive information irresponsibly

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions or access that violate these guidelines in order to maintain a safe and respectful environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, communication channels and contributions related to this project.
